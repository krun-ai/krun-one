# Krun One v1 — candidate tool/function routing sources after the xLAM exclusion

Date: 2026-09-26. Technical survey (HF API + cards + READMEs), **not legal advice**. Anything marked
**[unverified]** must be confirmed before ingestion. This feeds the future `KRUN_V1_TOOL_DATA_REPORT.md`, which is due
before the final fine-tune.

## Main finding

No dataset is **real + commercially clean + rich in distinct real APIs + non-English** at the same time.

* The license-clean, human-written sets are intent and task-oriented-dialogue corpora (hundreds of intents).
* The sets with thousands of tools are all LLM-synthetic, and most descend from xLAM or ToolBench, or were generated
  by models whose terms are problematic.

## Excluded because they descend from xLAM (same rule as xlam-irrelevance)

* `nvidia/When2Call`: the training set was generated from xLAM-60k, and the **test set comes from BFCL** (which is
  protected). Its Spanish port `aliaboost_when2call_es` is excluded with it.
* `nvidia/Nemotron-Agentic-v1`, `tool_calling` subset: contains xLAM tools (`is_leap_year`, `geocode_city`, …) and
  RapidAPI/ToolBench tools.
* `argilla/Synth-APIGen-v0.1` (seeded from xLAM) and `mhaugestad/tool-selection` (xLAM tools).

## Excluded because of license terms

* ToolBench/ToolLLM: "research and educational purposes only".
* APIGen-MT-5k: CC-BY-NC.
* NexusRaven function-call definitions: CC-BY-NC-SA.
* API-Blend: data not released.
* ToolAlpaca and GPT4Tools: license unclear, generated by OpenAI models.

## Estimated real pool after adoption

| | before (with xLAM) | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| real human routing queries | MASSIVE + BANKING77 | + ~250–350k (SGD, MTOP, Leyzer, CLINC, TM, SNIPS) **[estimate]** |
| distinct real intents/tools | ~140 intents | ~550–650 intents/services, roughly ~300 truly distinct functions across ~40 domains |
| synthetic tool breadth | xLAM ~3.6k + ToolACE | ToolACE ~26.5k (synthetic) |

**Consequence for the plan.** Real, human queries will cover function routing mostly at the level of intents and
services (tens to hundreds of functions per domain). Breadth across thousands of distinct APIs will come from
ToolACE plus local synthetic generation on top of real, clean schemas (SGD, and APIs.guru after per-spec checks).
Clean candidates like these are the reason the synthetic pilot (2k items, with an independent verifier) comes before
any volume generation.
